## Evidence boundary

The source-code review establishes what the prototype can do, but it is not user research. The public sources establish competitor offers and the existence or scope of standards; they do not establish demand, willingness to pay, or conformity. No interviews, usage analytics, conversion rates, market-size estimates, or validated prices for this product are available yet. Consequently, this package does not claim a market size or a viable price.

Before building a standards-labeled template, obtain the relevant standard text or an institution-approved specification, record its edition and locale, and test the generated document against it. A useful profile may be **inspired by** a standard without claiming certification or formal compliance.
