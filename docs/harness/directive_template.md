# Directive — <your product>

<!--
The directive is what your product is FOR, in one paragraph, plus the few invariants that make it that
product and not another. It is written once, before the first wave, and it does not change during the
loop: simulated users will keep asking for things that would quietly turn your product into a different
one, and this file is what the triage step says no with.

How to write it (15 minutes):
- Start from your brief (the one-page "what and for whom"), not from your screens.
- One paragraph, 3–5 sentences: who it serves, what they get, in what form, and who stays in control.
- Then 3–6 invariants. Each must be checkable on a screen or in a test ("every X shows Y"), not a value
  ("simple", "delightful"). If you cannot imagine a change that would break it, it is not an invariant.
- Leave out features. A directive that names a button has stopped being a directive.

Change it only outside the loop, as a decision of its own (and say so in your report): a wave that
rewrites the directive cannot be compared with the wave before it.
-->
