## What must not change

- I1: The student's course shows each part as its own item in one top-to-bottom stream in the order taught; the Due strip, the Your N minutes card and the day list sit above the stream and link into it, and nothing in this wave replaces the stream with one long page or re-sorts it.
- I2: Every part, in the stream, in the day list and in the teacher's preview, shows its verification badge, which reads the same to every student, and every part that has a Check me keeps it.
- I3: Everything a student marks (got it, Unclear, not now, a Check me answer) is visible to that student and can be changed or withdrawn by them, and no situation adds a score, rank or 'behind' label computed from those marks.
- I4: Students cannot add, reorder or remove the course's parts or homework; their questions to the library stay their own and remain readable by the teaching team, and every audience label says so truthfully.
- I5: Levels, budgets and personalisation change only the form, the length and the order of extras; what a part says, the homework and its deadlines are the same for every student.
