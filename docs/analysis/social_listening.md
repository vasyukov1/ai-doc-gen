# Social listening instructions

Input: permissioned interview notes or an export of public, dated posts about software documentation, course reports, architecture documentation, handover, and PDF deliverables.

1. Remove duplicates, bot-like promotions, link-only posts, and irrelevant discussions; report counts at every step.
2. Group by actual task and artifact: academic submission, client handover, internal knowledge transfer, compliance-oriented report, and other groups found in the data.
3. Extract recurring pains and workarounds with short exact quotes and source links. Remove personal identifiers.
4. Count mentions and negative experiences per group, with the denominator and search method visible.
5. Identify groups absent from the data. Public discussions are not a representative sample of buyers.
6. List findings that would change the first profile or the required editing workflow.

No corpus has been collected for this analysis. Do not produce topic shares or quotations until source records exist.
