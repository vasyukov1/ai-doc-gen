# Deep research prompt — one uncertain input

Use this prompt for one decision-critical unknown, for example: "Which exact documents do software students in a named European programme submit with a final project?"

---
Research **<one question>** for **<audience, institution or country, and year>**.

1. Find primary sources: official programme rules, standard catalog entries, product documentation, published pricing pages, or original studies as appropriate.
2. For each claim, give its definition, date, jurisdiction, source URL, and any population or sample behind it.
3. Show conflicting requirements or figures separately. Explain edition, method, and audience differences.
4. State what is unknown. Do not fill gaps with plausible-sounding requirements.
5. Identify the next real interview, artifact, or experiment needed to make the decision.

Return a source table, a bounded conclusion, and unresolved questions.
---

Before adding a result to [evidence_ledger.csv](evidence_ledger.csv), open the primary sources and verify the exact claim. A standards catalog abstract is evidence of scope, not evidence that a proposed template conforms to the full standard.
